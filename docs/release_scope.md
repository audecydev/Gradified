# Release scope

The present grading-engine candidate includes bounded CHOICE, DIRECT and unitless NUMERIC evaluation; supported DIRECT/NUMERIC paper composition; guarded FIRST/REGRADE/RETRY mechanisms; rubric/currentness checks; provider accounting and recovery boundaries; teacher decision custody; and conditional release mechanisms.

The broader SRS also describes mathematical derivations, units, diagrams, arbitrary mixed examinations, fully calibrated review and complete official teacher correction. Those are not claimed as completed engine capabilities. A representation in the evidence schema or an open extension interface is not evidence of a working evaluator.

The application team owns school accounts, scans and OCR runtime, real provider deployment, durable databases, HTTP APIs, student/teacher screens and actual publication. The engine still requires evidence that these adapters meet its trust and idempotency contracts.

Review the requirement-by-requirement boundary in [SRS traceability](srs_traceability.md).
